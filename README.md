<p>Medal of Honor Airborne Unofficial Remaster is my attempt at the remaster Airborne never got. It's a mod loader and 17 mods for the PC version that go through pretty much every part of the game: a new HUD, 1:1 damage, harder AI that you can tune per difficulty, lean, hold breath and stamina, suppression, ragdolls, a proper look for every map, longer view distance and reworked tanks. Plus a settings app so you can switch any of it off.</p>
<p>I built it around my own Expert playthroughs, so out of the box it's on the hard and realistic side. If that's not your thing, almost all of it can be turned down or off.</p>
<p>This is a fan project. It's not official and EA / DICE have nothing to do with it. There is a lot of new modifications and features. Most things have been changed so make sure to OVERWRITE your previous install. Please let me know if you encounter any bugs/issues. There is also a Testing Only category in the settings app which is OFF by default. If you would like to use it, give it a try and let me know.</p>
<p>Need help or just want to talk? Join my Discord: <a href="https://discord.gg/rnA3v3zaeh">discord.gg/rnA3v3zaeh</a></p>
<h2>DIFFICULTY</h2>
<p>Everyone takes the same damage from the same gun. You, your squad, the Germans, the Italians. Your headshots and the enemy's kill a soldier outright, and a helmet won't save him (it still flies off). The difficulty then decides how much punishment you can take:</p>
<p><strong>Casual</strong> - enemy AI a quarter of the way to the pack's harder AI. You have 2.5x health and the whole bar comes back 5 seconds after you get hurt.</p>
<p><strong>Normal</strong> - enemy AI three quarters of the way to the pack's harder AI. Normal health, and 10 seconds after you get hurt the quarter of the bar you're in fills back up.</p>
<p><strong>Expert</strong> - the full harder AI. Half health, no healing at all and no HUD. What you lose stays lost.</p>
<p>Enemy numbers are the game's own on Casual and Normal, and doubled on Expert (the Enemy Population mod).</p>
<p>Some enemy shots miss you on purpose on the lower difficulties (50% on Casual, 25% on Normal, none on Expert), and enemies far away take a few seconds to get their shots onto you (5 s on Casual, 2.5 s on Normal). Again, nothing like that on Expert.</p>
<h2>THE ENEMY</h2>
<p>They engage from further out, use cover better, peek less and push harder. Now and then they move to new cover, firing as they go, and when they get close they stand and fight instead of running past you. No more stopping to do hand signals in the middle of a fight either. They only see the 60 degrees in front of them, so no more turning on the spot to shoot you in the back. They also split their fire between you and your squad instead of all piling onto you.</p>
<p>Enemies and your squad move at a steady 500 whether they're walking or sprinting (the game's own is 500 / 600).</p>
<p>The Nazi Storm Elite are an actual threat now. They take 20x the punishment of a normal soldier, and on Normal and Expert they jog instead of plodding along. Headshots won't drop them in one either. Apart from that they're left exactly as the game made them, same AI and same aim.</p>
<h2>YOUR SQUAD</h2>
<p>With everyone doing full damage, your squad was clearing half the fights before you even got there. Now they're 50% worse shots, engage from half the distance, half their bullets miss and their hits do half damage. Their headshots don't one-shot enemies either. They keep the enemy busy, you do the killing. All of it has its own page in the settings app if you want them back to normal.</p>
<h2>TANKS</h2>
<p>Tank crews are a lot more dangerous and a lot more believable:</p>
<ul>
<li>They spot you from up to 150 m and check for targets several times a second. A tank that drives a route stops to fight after seeing you for 2.5 seconds and turns its gun on you after 5; a tank that stays put takes 2.5 seconds. Their shells fly twice as fast, hit twice as hard and blast twice as wide, and the hull machine gun opens up as soon as it sees you. On Expert they keep firing where they last saw you after you break line of sight.</li>
<li>The crews watch all round, so sneaking up behind a tank won't save you. Get right up against it, below where they can look, and it can't see you.</li>
<li>The main gun has a realistic reload and a slow 15 degree a second turret. It won't aim at you when you're right up close, but a moving tank will happily drive over you instead.</li>
<li>Tanks that drive a route can stop to fight you, pull up now and then, drive on to get a better angle when their shot is blocked, and swing the turret back to the front once there's nobody left.</li>
<li>Hit a tank and it stops, and if it can't see you the turret sweeps around looking for you. Wreck its tracks and the engine keeps running until you finish it off.</li>
<li>Tanks are heard from much further away and never go quiet in a big fight.</li>
</ul>
<h2>WEAPONS AND MOVEMENT</h2>
<ul>
<li>Lean on Q / E, from the hip or down the sights, and you can walk while leaning. Numpad lean keys work with Num Lock on or off.</li>
<li>Hold breath on Left Alt. Works on iron sights and scopes. On a scope it slows the game down for a moment, like the upgrade slow-mo.</li>
<li>Stamina, shared between sprinting and holding your breath. Sprint it dry and you're walking until you get it back.</li>
<li>Breathing sway when you aim, worse when you move or after a sprint, better crouched.</li>
<li>Hip fire actually scatters now and every gun kicks harder.</li>
<li>MG nests are a handful. They spray 5x wider and kick twice as hard, and the enemy gunners on them spray twice as wide.</li>
<li>Reload a box-magazine gun (Thompson, MP40, BAR, StG 44 or Gewehr 43) early and the rounds left in the magazine go with it. Pistols (endless ammo) and guns loaded a round at a time keep their rounds. Barrels smoke after a few rifle shots or a long burst.</li>
<li>Every gun kicks half as much while you're crouched.</li>
<li>Grenades are twice as loud and deeper, and their blast reaches twice as far. Your grenades, melee and every other hit can hurt your own squad too.</li>
<li>Suppression. Rounds cracking past you blur and darken the edges of the screen and jolt the camera, and when it gets really heavy your hearing goes and your ears ring.</li>
<li>Ragdolls that fall depending on where they were hit, with the game's own death animation about one time in four. Bodies are heavy, they never hang in the air or fly off, and they stick around instead of vanishing when you look away.</li>
<li>Badly hurt, you hear slow, heavy breathing and your heartbeat.</li>
<li>Movement with some weight to it: you build up to speed, the gun trails a little behind your movements, the camera catches up a moment later and moves with your steps, the lean eases in and out, and the view eases to a stop after you move the mouse. Each part can be turned down or off.</li>
<li>Weapon upgrades no longer slow everything down or blur the screen, and they don't show up on your squad's guns any more.</li>
</ul>
<h2>HUD</h2>
<p>A new HUD that keeps the game's own look, its brackets, the red cross and a steel-blue health bar, with spaced out capitals and italic serif type.</p>
<ul>
<li>A compass along the top with your current objectives and how far away they are, your squad as green dots and enemies who fire as red dots that fade out (within 100 m).</li>
<li>Health as four segments with how you're doing written above it: Fit for duty, Lightly wounded, Wounded, Seriously wounded. It goes red and pulses when you're nearly dead.</li>
<li>Your weapon, its upgrade stars, the ammo and your grenades. Handguns show their unlimited reserve as an infinity sign.</li>
<li>A small crosshair that shows where your shots will actually land, traced from the gun, not the middle of the screen.</li>
<li>A stamina bar, grenade warnings, objective notices, and stars for how well you landed after a jump.</li>
<li>New scopes with a sharp eyepiece, and a blurred world around them instead of a black screen.</li>
<li>Prefer the game's own HUD? Switch the new one off and you can move every part of it (compass, ammo, grenades, health and more) to any corner, edge or the centre, nudge it into place and make it bigger for 4K. Loading screens can be made bigger too.</li>
<li>No HUD at all on Expert.</li>
</ul>
<h2>GRAPHICS AND SOUND</h2>
<p>Every map has its own look now, each with its own page in the settings app. Husky is a warm night with a light haze, Avalanche cold with fog, Neptune and Der Flakturm grey and stormy with thunder and lightning, Market Garden dusty and Varsity has ash falling from the burning factory. On top of that there's ambient occlusion, sharpening, a film-style colour grade, better bloom and lens flare, light shafts, film grain, motion blur only on fast turns, and clouds and wind streaks rushing past on parachute jumps. There's also tone mapping, depth of field on iron sights, shell shock, barrel heat haze, lens dirt, fog glow, breath frost on Avalanche, wet ground reflections and contact shadows, plus SMAA anti-aliasing. The game runs on Vulkan through DXVK (you can switch it back to Direct3D 9), and the 60 frames a second cap is lifted.</p>
<p>View distance is 10x further for buildings, scenery and detail, and the field of view is wider (120, 105 aiming, 135 sprinting). All adjustable.</p>
<p>You'll also hear a battle going on around you: distant artillery, machine guns, rifle fire and planes overhead, all using the game's own sounds. It goes quiet indoors and fades in on the way down from a jump. Every gunshot echoes a little off the buildings and hills around it, and gunshots carry five times further. On High Sound Quality the game can play 150 sounds at once instead of 91, and tank sounds are never the ones it drops.</p>
<h2>SETTINGS APP</h2>
<p>Everything in the pack is in one window. Every option has a short explanation of what it does, there's a Reset button for each page, and Launch Game starts the game for you. It also has the game's own launcher settings and full key bindings, with two keys per action, so you can put weapon switching and scope zoom on the mouse wheel together. Tanks that stay put and tanks that drive a route each have their own copy of every tank setting. And it plays music while it's open, with a volume control in the top left.</p>
<p>Check for Updates, at the top of the window, looks for a newer version of the pack. If there is one it downloads it with a progress bar, installs it and opens the app again, so you don't have to come back here for every update. Updating can reset your mod settings to the new version's defaults.</p>

<h2>INSTALLING</h2>
<ol>
<li>Back up UnrealEngine3\MOHAGame\Config and MOHAGame\Splash\Splash.bmp in your game folder.</li>
<li>Copy Binaries and MOHAGame from the zip's Game Folder into UnrealEngine3 and say yes to replace. dinput8.dll has to end up next to MOHA.exe.</li>
<li>Move the six MOHA*.ini files out of Documents\EA Games\Medal of Honor Airborne(tm)\Config so the game makes fresh ones. You'll have to set your resolution and keys again.</li>
<li>Start the game. You'll see REMASTERED on the splash screen and a badge on the main menu.</li>
<li>Optional but recommended: grab the 4GB patch and run it once.</li>
</ol>
<p>The full guide, uninstall steps and troubleshooting are in the README. To turn the whole thing off quickly, just delete Binaries\dinput8.dll.</p>

<h2>ANTIVIRUS / BROWSER WARNINGS</h2>
<p>Your browser or antivirus might flag the download. It's a false positive and the mod is completely safe. It happens because the mod loader is a dinput8.dll that loads itself into the game when it starts, and that's the same trick a lot of game mods and fixes use. Nothing in the pack touches anything outside the game folder apart from the game's own settings files in Documents. If it gets blocked, allow it in your browser or antivirus, or check it on VirusTotal yourself first.</p>
