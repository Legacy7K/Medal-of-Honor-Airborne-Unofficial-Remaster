# Changelog

Everything that's changed since the last release. A lot of settings files have changed, so make sure to OVERWRITE your previous install.

## New

- HUD Layout. With the Newsreel HUD off, every part of the game's own HUD (compass, gun and ammo, grenades, health, stance, landing grade and medals, messages and action prompts) can be put in any corner, edge or the centre, and fine-tuned across and up and down. Find it under Interface > HUD Layout.
- HUD size goes up to 4.00 (twice the old maximum) for 4K screens and TVs, and now sizes the game's own HUD too when the Newsreel HUD is off. Each part grows from its own corner or edge of the screen, so nothing overlaps. It never applies in the main menu.
- Loading screen size. The loading screens, and the quote and title cards before a mission, can be drawn bigger.
- Compass dots on the Newsreel HUD. Your squad shows as green dots, and an enemy who fires shows as a red dot that fades out over a few seconds, within 100 m.
- Grenades. Every grenade, hand and rifle grenades, yours and the enemy's, is twice as loud and deeper, and its blast reaches twice as far. The damage at the centre is the game's own. Grenades have their own page under Weapons.
- Friendly fire covers everything you do now: grenades, rifle grenades, rockets, charges and melee can hurt your squad too.
- Tank shells fly twice as fast, hit twice as hard and blast twice as wide.
- The tank's hull machine gun opens fire as soon as it can see you or your squad.
- Moving tanks have two timers of their own: how long they see you before they stop to fight, and how long before they turn the gun on you. They treat your squad the same as you, and the random stops are gone. Stationary tanks use the same "Targets you after seeing you for" timer.
- Skip the intros. One setting skips everything before the main menu: the title screen with the ESRB notice and the EA logo intro with its music.
- Rounds lost on an early reload now covers the Gewehr 43 as well as the Thompson, MP40, BAR and StG 44. Pistols (endless ammo) and guns loaded a round at a time keep their rounds.

## Fixes

- Ragdolls no longer fly up into the air when they're killed, or float up into the sky and cartwheel in slow motion later.
- The shadow that crawled across walls on Husky as you moved is gone.
- The skip intro options didn't work. Fixed, and now one setting.
- Switching Coloured gun pictures off now really puts the game's blueprint pictures back in the class selection.
- A crash while a mission loads with Coloured gun pictures on is fixed.
- Drop-down lists in the settings app with seven choices showed the wrong seventh choice.
- Settings for mods that aren't installed no longer show up on other pages.

## New Defaults

- Double the enemies on Expert is on (Casual and Normal keep the game's own numbers).
- Enemy shots miss you 25% of the time on Normal (was 10%).
- Enemies see the 60 degrees in front of them and 45 degrees up and down (was 75 / 75).
- Tanks check for targets every 0.25 s when they stay put and every 0.5 s when they drive (was 2.5 s). Stationary tanks target you after seeing you for 2.5 s (was 1.5 s), moving tanks stop after 2.5 s and target you after 5 s.
- The tank's hull gun watches the 180 degrees in front of the tank (was all round), and the blind spot right up close to a tank is 10 m (was 8 m).
- Tanks are heard twice as far away (was ten times).
- The in-world crosshair is on.
- HUD size is 1.00 (was 0.75).
- Sway down the sights is 0.25 degrees (was 0.5), and scopes drift 1.25x (was 1.5x).
- Aiming recoil is 1.5x (was 2.5x). MG nests kick 1.5x (was 2x) and enemy gunners on them spray 2.5x as wide (was 2x).
- Stamina lasts 30 s of sprinting (was 15 s) and fills back up in 15 s (was 30 s).
- Movement: lean eases in over 0.5 s (was 0.3 s), the gun lags behind a little more, the camera weight is 0.2 from the hip and 0.1 down the sights, and turning eases to a stop over 0.3 s from the hip and 0.15 s aiming.
- Grenade explosions are deeper, and the heartbeat when you're badly hurt is a little louder.
- The intros are skipped.

## Settings App

- New pages: HUD Layout (Interface), Grenades (Weapons) and Storm Elite MG42 (Testing Only).
- Suppression moved to Battlefield, next to Bodies and Bullet Holes.
- Saving Coloured gun pictures now switches the pictures straight away, so it shows the next time you start the game.
- Everything under Testing Only is off by default.
- The settings app has no Developer Menu options (the Developer Menu is a separate download).
